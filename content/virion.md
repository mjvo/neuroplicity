## About Virion

*Virion* is an action-puzzle game centered around cellular biology. Players take control of a nanobot sent inside a human body to fight off a deadly rabies infection. Armed with a calcium gun and other bio-weapons, the nanobot fights through the body to save its host. Every object in the game is analogous to a real cellular structure, and players use this information to figure out how to progress. *Virion* aspires to be a fun and unique learning experience for anyone interested in biology. 
The team developed the first level of this multi-level game in 2016-2017.
